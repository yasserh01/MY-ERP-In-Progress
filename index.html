User manual

Learning the ERP, one module at a time

This manual walks through every module of the company ERP: what each screen is for, every field on its forms, the options in each list, the buttons you will see, and the order in which to do things.

How to use this manual

Pick a module from the menu on the left. Each module page starts with a short explanation and a map of all its screens, grouped the same way as in the ERP (Masters & Settings, Transactions and Reports). Under the map, every screen is described with:

  • What it is for and when you use it.
  • How to use it: the steps in order.
  • A screenshot of the screen and of its entry form. Select any picture to enlarge it.
  • A field table: every field on the form, its type, the choices in each drop-down list, and what to enter. Fields marked required must be filled before you can save; fields marked automatic are filled by the system.
  • Buttons that are special to the screen, and Good to know notes.

Use the search box at the top to jump straight to any screen, field or topic. New users should read Working with the ERP first: it explains the parts every screen shares.

First-time setup: the order that works

The modules depend on each other. Setting them up in this order avoids empty drop-down lists and missing accounts later.

  1. Company details. < Company Settings>>: name in English and Arabic, TRN, address, emirate and currency. These appear at the top of every printed document.
  2. Company mode. < Company Mode>>: keep Single company unless you run several companies of a group in one system.
  3. Chart of accounts and posting accounts. < Chart of Accounts>> → Load starter chart, then review < Account Mapping>> so each posting key points to a ledger.
  4. Organisation. Branches, Departments, Designations, Cost Centres, Units, Currencies and Payment Terms in Master Data.
  5. Banks and cash. Bank Accounts, Cash Boxes and Petty Cash Holders in < Masters & Settings>>.
  6. Parties and items. Suppliers, Customers, Item Categories, Items, Service Items and Stores.
  7. Projects. Projects, Cost Types, WBS / Activities and BOQ budgets.
  8. People. Employees (with documents and payroll details), Payroll Settings, Payroll Projects and the Holiday Calendar.
  9. Opening balances. Opening balances on the chart of accounts, opening stock, the asset register and employee opening leave and EOSB.
  10. Security. Create standard roles in < Role Permissions>>, add users, set approval rules, then turn on sign-in in < Login & Security>>.
  11. Backup. Create the first backup and download the ZIP file. Turn on automatic backups.
Where your data is keptThe ERP runs in your web browser and stores its data in that browser on that computer. Another computer or another browser starts empty. To move data, use < Backup Management>>: create a backup on one computer and import it on the other. Clearing the browser's site data deletes the ERP data, so keep downloaded backups somewhere safe.

Working with the ERP

Every module is built from the same parts. Learn them once here and every screen in the manual will feel familiar.

The home screen and the top bar

The home screen shows one large tile per module. Select a tile to open the module. Each module opens a hub with three cards: Masters & Settings (the set-up records the module uses), Transactions (daily entries and processing) and Reports. Selecting a card lists its screens in small groups.

The home screen also has a Find a screen box: type part of a name (supplier, payroll, VAT, stock…) to jump straight to any screen. The bar at the top of the ERP is always visible:

Row buttons

Entry forms

Forms open in a window over the list. The title shows New or Edit, the current status and the document number.

  • Sections. Fields are grouped under headings such as General information, Lines, Additional details and Remarks. Select a heading to fold or unfold it. Additional details starts folded and shows how many fields it holds.
  • Required fields carry a red *. The form will not save until they are filled.
  • Automatic numbers and codes. Document numbers (LPO-000001) and master codes (SUP-0001, ITM-0001, EMP-0001) are created by the system and cannot be typed. Their format is set in Document Numbering.
  • Drop-down lists are filled from the masters. If a list is empty, create the master record first (for example a supplier before an LPO). Long lists are searchable: start typing to narrow them.
  • Keyboard: Ctrl+S saves, Esc closes without saving.

Line items

Purchase, sales and stock documents have a Lines table. Each line has a Type that decides what the second column lists:

Inventory
A stock item from the Items master. It updates stock quantities and values in the chosen store.
Asset
An asset from the Asset Register. Its cost goes to the asset's cost account.
Service
A service item. Linked to a project and activity, it becomes project cost.
GL Account
Any posting ledger from the chart of accounts, for charges that are neither stock nor assets (invoices and notes only).

Enter Qty, Rate, Disc % and VAT % (5 by default). Project, Activity and Cost centre on the line carry the cost to the right place. Source qty shows, for converted lines, the quantity on the earlier document, what was already used and what remains. The bar under the table shows Subtotal, Discount, Taxable amount, VAT and Total. Use + Add line to add a row and × to remove one.

The life of a document

Transactions move through statuses. The buttons on each row change to match.

Draft→ Submit →Submitted→ Approve / Post →Approved / Posted→ Convert →Partially …→Fully … / Closed
Draft
Saved but not final. Can be edited or deleted. Documents that post directly (LPO, GRN, invoices, notes) go straight from Draft to Approve / Post; others need Submit first.
Submitted / Under Review
Waiting for approval. Someone whose role has the Approve / post permission sees Approve or Post.
Approved
Accepted, with no accounting yet (requisitions, orders, quotations). It can now be converted to the next document.
Posted
The accounting entry is in the General Ledger. A posted document cannot be edited; it can only be reversed.
Partially / Fully …
Shows how much has moved to the next document: Partially Received, Fully Invoiced, Partially Paid, Paid.
Rejected
Sent back. Edit it and submit again.
Cancelled
Voided before posting. Kept for the record with the reason.
Closed
Finished. No further conversions.
Approval limitsIf an Approval Rule covers the document type and amount, only the named role can approve it. A role without Approve / post permission never sees those buttons. Postings into a month that is closed in Period Control are refused until the period is reopened.

Printing and PDF

All documents print in one company format: company name and address, Arabic name, a title badge (TAX INVOICE, PURCHASE ORDER…), the number, date and party strip, an information box, the line items with VAT, the totals, the amount in words, a computer-generated note, three signature boxes and a generated-on footer.

In the print preview, choose A4 Portrait or A4 Landscape, choose which signature boxes to show, then select Print / Save as PDF. To make a PDF, pick Save as PDF as the printer in the browser's print window. Reports have their own Print, PDF and Excel buttons.

Importing and exporting

Master lists (suppliers, customers, items, employees, and more) have Template and Import. Download the template, keep the heading row exactly as it is, add one record per row, save as CSV and import. The timesheet and some payroll screens have their own Excel templates. Every list and report can be exported with Excel / CSV or Excel.

Users, roles and what you can see

Your role decides which modules you can open, whether you can approve and post, whether you can delete drafts, and whether you can see salary and bank data. Screens you are not allowed to use do not appear in the hubs; opening one directly shows Access denied. Salary and IBAN fields show •••• to roles without salary permission. See Role Permissions.

Saving and backing up

Every save is stored at once in the browser; there is no separate save-to-server step. Because the data lives in the browser, regular backups matter. Create a backup at least daily in Backup Management, download the ZIP and keep copies outside the computer. The working-data meter there shows how much of the browser's storage (about 5 MB) is in use.

Master Data

The company-wide records every other module reads: companies, branches, departments, designations, cost centres, the chart of accounts, tax codes, currencies, units and payment terms.

Master Data is set up once and then changed only occasionally. Everything you choose from a drop-down list in a transaction comes from here or from a module's own masters. Set these up before you start entering transactions, and keep names consistent: the name you give a department or cost centre is exactly what appears in every report.

Some masters belong to one module and are explained there: Suppliers in Purchase, Customers in Sales, Items, Categories and Stores in Inventory, Employees in HR & Payroll, Bank Accounts, Cash Boxes and Petty Cash Holders in Finance.

Chart of Accounts

Master Data › Screens › Accounts, tax & units › Chart of Accounts

The list of all accounts the company posts to, arranged as a tree: five fixed elements (1 Assets, 2 Liabilities, 3 Equity, 4 Income, 5 Expenses), groups you create under them (for example Current Assets › Cash on Hand), and ledgers under the groups. Only ledgers can be posted to. Codes are created automatically from the position in the tree and never change.

The screen is split into the Balance Sheet accounts (elements 1–3) and the Profit and Loss Statement accounts (elements 4–5). Each row shows the account, its control or analysis setting, and its current balance.

Starting outSelect Load starter chart to load a ready chart for a UAE engineering and contracting company: bank and cash, receivables, retention, VAT input and output, inventory, CIP and fixed assets, payables, GRN payables, accruals, salaries payable, leave, air-ticket and EOSB provisions, equity, contract revenue, cost of revenue, and the expense groups. Accounts that already exist are left untouched. Then rename, add or deactivate accounts to suit the company.
How to use it
  1. To add a group, select New Group (or + Group on the element row). Choose the Parent (an element or another group) and enter the name. For groups under Expenses that hold direct project costs, set Direct cost group to Yes so their ledgers count as cost of sales.
  2. To add a ledger, select New Ledger, choose its group, enter the name and, where needed, its control type (Bank, Cash, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable…) and analysis type.
  3. Use Tree view / List view, Expand all and Collapse all to move around the chart.
  4. Select Opening balances to enter the balances brought forward on the go-live date.
  5. Use Print, PDF or Excel to share the chart.

Module guide

Projects

Set up each project, build its Bill of Quantities and budgets, certify the work done, bill the client through payment certificates, control cost against budget and close each month and finally the project.

The Projects module is the backbone of contracting work. Every purchase, stock issue, payroll hour, petty cash expense and sales invoice can carry a Project and an Activity, and this module brings them together against the budgets.

The two budgets. Each BOQ line carries what the client pays (quantity × contract rate: the sales budget) and what the work costs you (material, labour, equipment, subcontract and overhead per unit: the cost budget). The difference is the expected margin.

From work to cash. The engineer certifies the design quantity, the project manager or QS certifies the installed quantity on a Measurement Sheet; the lower of the two is billable. A Payment Certificate (IPC) bills that quantity; once approved, revenue is recognised (IFRS 15, output method) and the tax invoice is raised in Sales.

Module guide

Purchase

Suppliers and the full procurement cycle: requisition, quotations and bid comparison, purchase order, goods or service receipt, supplier invoice with three-way match, returns, notes and payment.

PR→Bid tabulation→LPO→GRN→Purchase invoice→Payment→Supplier SOA

Every step creates the next document from the previous one, so quantities and prices are typed once and the link between documents is kept (see Document Flow in the Actions menu). Partial quantities are allowed at every step: an LPO can be received in several GRNs, and a GRN invoiced in several invoices.

Accounting. The LPO has no accounting entry. The GRN posts Dr Inventory / project cost / asset, Cr GRN Payables. The purchase invoice clears GRN Payables, adds Input VAT and credits the supplier (Accounts Payable). The payment debits the supplier and credits the bank. Services received on a GRN with a project and activity go straight to project cost.

Module guide

Sales

Customers and the cycle from quotation to cash: sales orders, deliveries, tax invoices, credit notes, returns and receipts, plus contract billing, revenue recognition, retention and credit control.

Quotation→Sales order→Delivery (GDN)→Sales invoice→Receipt→Customer SOA

Trading sales follow Quotation → Sales Order → Delivery Note (GDN) → Sales Invoice → Customer Receipt, each created from the one before with partial quantities allowed. Contract work is billed through Payment Certificates (IPC) in the Projects module or Progress Certificates here, and revenue on over-time contracts is recognised by the monthly Revenue Recognition run.

Accounting. A posted GDN moves the goods out of stock at their system cost (first in, first out) and records the sale as GDN Receivables (delivered, not yet invoiced). The invoice moves it to the customer (Accounts Receivable) and adds Output VAT. The receipt debits the bank and credits the customer.

Credit control. Each customer has a credit limit and credit days. When a new order or invoice would exceed them, the system blocks it (an approver can override) or only warns, according to the customer's Credit Control setting.

Module guide

Inventory

Items, categories and stores; receiving, issuing, transferring, adjusting and counting stock; writing stock down to net realisable value; reorder planning, valuation and stock reports.

Stock comes in through GRNs (from suppliers), Opening Stock and Stock Receipts (returns from site, client-supplied material, production). It goes out through Stock Issues to projects, departments or accounts, Material Consumption, and Delivery Notes to customers. Transfers move it between stores, Adjustments and Stock Counts correct it, and NRV write-downs provide for stock worth less than its cost.

Costing. Stock is valued by the cost method in Inventory Settings: FIFO (oldest cost first, the default) or weighted average. Every document shows the cost it will use; issued cost cannot be typed over.

Accounts. Each movement posts to the stock account of the item; if the item has none, the category's account, and if that is blank too, the default in Inventory Settings. Use Fill missing accounts on the Items screen to complete them in one go.

Fixed Assets

The asset register with documents and components, capitalisation of cost under IAS 16, monthly depreciation, transfers between locations and custodians, and disposal.

An asset is first created in the Asset Register (or by a Capitalisation). Its cost is built up from the GRN, purchase invoice or Capitalisation lines that select it, so the register always agrees with the purchases. HR & Payroll

Employees and their documents, the monthly payroll from timesheet to WPS payment, additions, deductions, loans, salary changes, leave and rejoin, EOSB and final settlement, HR letters, appraisals, recruitment, onboarding, renewals and clearance.

Employee master→Payroll projects & shifts→Monthly parameters→Timesheet→Payroll run→Approval→JV posting→WPS payment→Reports

The monthly payroll runs in a fixed order: approve the Monthly Parameters (rest days, holidays, standard hours) → load and approve the Timesheet → approve any Additions, Deductions and Loans for the month → Payroll Run (1. Process, 2. Approve, 3. Create JV and post) → Payroll Payment by WPS, cheque or cash.

Where cost goes. Each employee has a default Payroll Project (a cost destination such as a project's WIP, admin or site overhead). Timesheet hours by payroll project and activity decide where salary and overtime are charged, so labour cost appears in project cost control.

Provisions. Each posted payroll also accrues annual leave, EOSB and other benefit provisions per employee. Annual Leave Settlement and Final Settlement release them. The Employee Control Statement shows every amount owed to or by each employee.

Rules and rates. Overtime rates and bases, leave accrual, the leave and EOSB divisors, the EOSB cap, sick-leave pay, notice and the final-settlement deadline all come from Payroll Settings, which lists the values the system starts with. Loan recovery is limited to 20% of salary per loan and 50% for all deductions together.

For groups of companies run in one ERP: staff cost, sponsorship and asset-usage recharges between companies, intercompany invoices, settlement and netting, transfer pricing, and consolidated statements.

This module appears only when Company Mode is set to Multiple companies and at least two companies are active. A company selector then appears in the top bar: every entry, report and print follows the company chosen, and All companies (Group view) opens the group screens.

Monthly routine. In Month-End Charges the system prepares the charges for payroll one company paid for another, sponsorship costs and asset usage; you review and post them as Intercompany Invoices (recorded in both companies at once), reconcile, and settle the net balance in Settlement and Netting. Consolidated statements eliminate all intercompany balances automatically.

Module guide

Finance

The general ledger and everything around it: posting accounts and controls, journals and vouchers, petty cash, banking and reconciliation, treasury (LC, LG, PDC, overdrafts, loans, deposits), period-end accruals, prepayments, leases and provisions, budgets, VAT, corporate tax, ICV and the financial statements.

Transaction→Accounting event→Posting rule→Journal→GL

Most entries reach the ledger automatically from other modules (see How accounting happens). Finance users check them, add what only Finance knows (accruals, provisions, bank charges, journals), reconcile the banks, close the period and report.

Controls. Account Mapping links each posting key to a ledger. Period Control closes months so nothing can be dated in them. Analysis Templates decide what extra detail is asked for on analysed accounts. Vouchers, transfers, accruals, budgets and corporate tax each have their own approval levels (Approval inbox), and posted entries are reversed, never deleted.

Month-end order. Post all module documents → run depreciation, payroll, project month-end → accruals, prepayment and lease postings, provisions → FX revaluation → bank reconciliations → check the control reconciliations (GRN payables, analysis, sales reconciliations) → trial balance and financial statements → close the period.

Glossary & FAQ

Terms used in the ERP

AP / AR
Accounts Payable (what the company owes suppliers) and Accounts Receivable (what customers owe the company).
Activity / WBS
A line of the project work breakdown structure. Costs and budgets are tracked per activity.
Analysis type
A setting on a ledger that asks for an extra breakdown (employee, vehicle, project, asset, supplier, customer or custom) whenever the ledger is posted.
BOQ
Bill of Quantities: the priced list of contract work, used for the contract value and cost budget of a project.
CIP
Capital work in progress: cost collected on an asset or project that is not finished yet.
Control account
A ledger that summarises a sub-ledger, such as Accounts Payable for all suppliers. Suppliers and customers sit under their control ledgers.
Cost centre
A part of the business used to group costs (a department, a site, a vehicle pool).
ECL
Expected credit loss under IFRS 9: an allowance for receivables that may not be collected, based on their age.
EOSB
End of service benefit (gratuity) under the UAE Labour Law: 21 days of basic salary per year for the first five years, 30 days per year after.
GDN
Goods Delivery Note: proof that goods left the store for the customer.
GRN
Goods Received Note: proof that goods or services were received from a supplier.
GRN Payable
A clearing liability for goods received but not invoiced yet. The purchase invoice clears it.
ICV
In-Country Value: the UAE programme that scores suppliers on local spend, used in tenders.
IPC
Interim Payment Certificate: the periodic certificate of work done that the client approves before billing.
JV
Journal Voucher: a manual accounting entry.
LC / LG
Letter of Credit and Letter of Guarantee issued by a bank.
LPO
Local Purchase Order sent to a supplier.
MOL / WPS
Ministry of Human Resources (labour) and the Wage Protection System through which salaries are paid by bank SIF file.
NRV
Net realisable value: stock is written down when it is worth less than its cost.
PDC
Post-dated cheque.
Posting
Writing a document's accounting entry into the General Ledger.
Retention
Part of a contract value held back by the client (or by you from a subcontractor) until completion or maintenance ends.
Reversal
A new journal that cancels a posted one. The original stays visible.
SOA
Statement of Account for a supplier or customer.
TRN
Tax Registration Number issued by the Federal Tax Authority.
Unbilled revenue
Work done for a customer that has not been invoiced yet (a contract asset).

Common questions

A drop-down list on a form is empty.

The list comes from a master. Create the master record first (for example a supplier, project, store or bank account), then reopen the form.

I cannot find the Approve or Post button.

Your role does not have the Approve / post permission, or an approval rule assigns this amount to another role. Ask the administrator.

The system says the period is closed.

Finance has locked that month in Period Control. Ask Finance to reopen it, or date the document in an open month.

I cannot edit a posted document.

Posted documents are locked. Use Actions → Void / Reverse, then copy the document, correct it and post again.

I cannot delete a document.

Delete is only for drafts with no postings and no later documents. Use Void / Cancel instead; the Actions menu explains why delete is blocked.

My data is missing on another computer.

Data is stored in each browser. Import the latest backup on that computer in Backup Management.

Salary figures show ••••.

Your role does not have Salary and bank data permission.

An account shows “(not mapped: …)” in the accounting entry.

A posting key has no ledger. Set it in Finance → Account Mapping.
